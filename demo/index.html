SIH internal prototype

Two forms. One private profile. Zero raw values for the planner.

These controlled pages exercise different field structures so the extension can demonstrate scanning, anonymized planning, local filling and confirmation without touching a real service.

Form A · Standard structure

National Scholarship Application

Explicit labels, common profile fields, course selection and applicant consent.

Form B · Mixed structure

Citizen Service Registration

Wrapping labels, ARIA metadata, placeholders, radio controls and a blocked OTP field.
Privacy boundary: the pages do not send form values anywhere. Submission is simulated locally, and the later extension confirmation guard will intercept the final action.
